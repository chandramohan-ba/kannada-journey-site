How it works

A clear path, not a heavy syllabus.

Kannada Journey is designed as a sequence of small, practical steps. You start with useful everyday language and gradually build towards clearer expression and deeper understanding.

What you can expect

The focus is on language you can actually use: at work, in neighbourhoods, clinics, cafés, yoga spaces, markets, homes, and everyday moments.

Practical, real-life Kannada

Learn sentences and expressions that fit your daily routines and interactions, not abstract grammar drills.

Guided progression

Move through programs in a logical order, with each stage building on the previous one.

Comfortable pace

Take your time. The materials are designed so you can revisit and reinforce without feeling rushed.

How the journey unfolds

The pathway is organised into six programs, grouped into three stages. Each stage builds on the previous one.

Programs 1–2

First spoken sentences and everyday use.
Begin with simple spoken patterns and extend Kannada across practical everyday situations and relationships.

Programs 3–4

How Kannada works and clearer expression of actions.
Understand how sentences are built and how spoken and written forms relate. Learn to express actions that are ongoing, completed, or experienced.

Programs 5–6

Richer conversations and real-world expression.
Go beyond simple sentences with advanced structures for natural spoken and written Kannada. Expand vocabulary through themes, newspapers, films, news, roleplay, discussion, and real-world practice.
